1. Body Profile Formula (Base: 2,620 ml)

2. Weather & Temperature Adjustment

Automatically adjusts your target for warm temperatures (>25°C) and humidity so you replace moisture lost to heat.

Divides your daily goal across 15, 30, or 60-minute intervals during waking hours and automatically resets intake at midnight.
