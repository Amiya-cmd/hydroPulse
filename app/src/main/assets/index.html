How Your Water Goal is Calculated

1. Compulsory Body Metrics (Age, Height, Weight)

Base intake uses Weight (kg) × Age Multiplier (40 ml/kg under 30 yrs, 35 ml/kg for 30–55 yrs, 30 ml/kg over 55 yrs) plus a Height surface modifier (+3 ml per cm above 165 cm).

2. Live GPS / Address Weather & Temperature

Warm and hot climates increase perspiration. Above 25°C adds +60 ml per °C, plus condition modifiers (+100 ml Sunny, +250 ml Humid, +400 ml Scorching).

3. 15 / 30 / 60-Minute Interval Quota

Your total daily water requirement is divided evenly across your chosen 15, 30, or 60-minute interval between your Wake-Up Start Time and Sleep End Time.
